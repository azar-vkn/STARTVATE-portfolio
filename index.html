Why it compounds

The ecosystem flywheel

Every new builder makes the graph denser, and a denser graph makes every recommendation better. The product gets more valuable as it grows, not more crowded.

  • 1
    More builders join to find people and opportunities around their idea.
  • 2
    More startups and ideas get mapped, making the ecosystem picture sharper.
  • 3
    More opportunities and data flow in — organisers and investors follow the builders.
  • 4
    Better AI recommendations emerge from a denser, more connected graph.
  • 5
    More successful connections — co-founders found, applications won, funding closed.
  • 6
    More value per user, which brings the next wave of builders in.

The defensibility isn't the interface — it's the graph. Intent data, match outcomes and ecosystem coverage compound into recommendations that a general-purpose search or a generic social network structurally cannot produce.
